# Starter House — Design Brief

A minimal, attractive two-storey rectangular house used as the `haus new --template
minimal` scaffold and the cold-start delight target (→ 02 §Verification).

- **Spatial program:** one living level, one bedroom level.
- **Budget:** n/a (template).
- **Climate:** cold (Minnesota, zone 6/7); PGH-class envelope target.
- **Style:** Nordic — muted palette, simple gable form.
- **Accessibility:** single-family, no specific requirements.
- **Must-haves:** closed wall loop per floor, one egress-capable bedroom window.
- **Dislikes:** gray-box walls (we show real framing).
