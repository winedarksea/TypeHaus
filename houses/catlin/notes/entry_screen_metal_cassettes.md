# W-BW-SCREEN metal cassette concept — 2026-11-02

**Design development; not a fabrication or construction release.** The east field uses
UNA-CLAD Bone White PVDF 0.050-inch aluminum, with the finished face at x=77.875 in,
1/4 in behind the timber post face. The west shear panel, corrugated face, posts, sill,
connections, open upper brace band and 81° door stop remain. The model removes east brick,
mortar, board, drainage mat, plywood and packing. The continuous WRB is shown behind a
0.690 in space, with segmental head/base flashing. Actual WRB support, end dams, perimeter
trim, clips, blocking and drainage/vent paths require detailing.

Stations along the south-to-north wall axis are 0.125–7.875, 13.625–40.5625,
40.6875–67.625 and 73.375–79.25 inches. The 1/8 in nominal perimeter/joint spaces are
coordination allowances; verify movement and fabrication tolerances. The face spans +2 to
+47.5 in, totaling 21.328 sf and approximately 15.0 lb of flat sheet at 2700 kg/m³,
before returns, stiffeners, clips, supports and flashing. The model's 24.87 plf wall
weight similarly excludes those unmodelled components and is **not a final design load**.

A provisional 48 × 96 in stock layout can fit all four blanks with provisional 1/2 in straight returns:
two 27.9375 × 46.5 in central blanks end-to-end in one strip, and 8.75 × 46.5 and
6.875 × 46.5 in end blanks side-by-side in the remaining strip. This is a nesting study,
not a cut list: bend deductions, kerf, edge defects, tolerances and actual support details
must be confirmed. Flashings and stiffeners need additional stock. One fixed datum per
cassette with sliding attachments elsewhere is required; isolate aluminum from treated wood
and dissimilar metals. Require a full representative formed sample. Provide aligned
supports and controlled attachment; broad flat panels can show oil canning.

The preliminary envelope rate is $9–16/sf material and $30–55/sf shop and installation
labour, or $832–1,514 on 21.328 sf, excluding separately priced WRB and any items not
covered by a fabricator's quote. This is an allowance, not a quote or a full assembly
comparison. The superseded brick field alone was budgeted at $25–46/sf, excluding its
backer, drainage, plywood, packing, and other layers; the scopes are different.

Before fabrication: dimension returns, all joints, clips, stiffeners, blocking, end dams,
flashings and drainage; select and verify fasteners and isolation; calculate panel/clip
wind strength, service deflection and thermal movement; add all component weights; rerun
sill, guard, shear, post-base, uplift and overturning checks with the complete load; model
and sweep every trim against the service door. Existing general structural checks do not
prove the cassette assembly. Confirm finished samples and supplier availability.
