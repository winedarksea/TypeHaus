# Access point geometry reference

The kitchen, studio and sheltered porch APs use the **Ubiquiti UniFi U6 Pro (U6-Pro)**
as a provisional geometry reference. Final radio selection remains open; this is a
dimensioned design reference rather than a coverage study or purchase decision.

[Ubiquiti technical specifications](https://techspecs.ui.com/unifi/wifi/u6-pro),
read 2026-11-16, publish a **197 mm diameter × 35 mm thick** polycarbonate enclosure,
ceiling/wall mounting with a stainless steel mount, IP54 protection, an operating
range of −30 to 60 °C and 13 W maximum consumption. The existing **15 W per AP**
PoE design allowance is retained pending final selection.

The shared generated model includes a shallow turned white housing, the blue central
status ring and a recessed mounting plate. Profile and ring proportions are visual
approximations from Ubiquiti's product images; the overall envelope uses the published
dimensions. Concealed connector pockets, fasteners and internal electronics are omitted.
The blue ring illustrates an operating unit, not simulated network state.

Ceiling faces point down, with their backs at the ceiling/soffit mounting plane. The
studio uses the same enclosure turned upright, its back against the wall finish and its
face toward the room. Viewer, GLB and IFC consume the same generated geometry.

The porch reference assumes the existing sheltered soffit location. IP54 is not a
general exposed wet-location specification; an exposed installation needs a separately
selected outdoor product and mounting detail.
