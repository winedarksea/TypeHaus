## Sheet notes

### General
- Flat heads on an L3x3x1/4 HDG angle lintel each, 4" bearing a side (IRC R703.8.3, Table R703.8.3.1). No arch.
- Ties: 2024 IRC Table R703.8.4(1) W2.8, 2 pintle legs, every tie on a stud, extra ties within 12" of each opening.
- The wythe is dry-bedded on the base flashing: no mortar bond to the beam. The court slides under it.

### Keyed
- [K1 @ host#layer:brick:out] Base bond break and flashing: steel sheet on the beam top under course 1, leg up the backup 2", drip 3/8" out.
- [K2] Weeps (IRC R703.8.6): open head joints, 3/16" or more, over the flashing and over each lintel, 33" o.c. at most.
- [K3] Lintel flashing (IRC R703.8.5): the same sheet lapped up the backup and out over the angle, ends turned up 2" as dams.
- [K4] Tie rows are bed joints, 24" o.c. at most: 16", 34 2/3", 48", 61 1/3", 85 1/3" over the base; the door head row is on the cripples.

# Notes

Rationale and sources: `sunken_garden_veneer_beam.md` §6l-§6m. The flashing sheet is the house's
brake-formed steel; stainless is the upgrade for wet alkaline mortar.
