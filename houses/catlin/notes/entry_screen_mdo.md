# W-BW-SCREEN — white painted MDO, 2026-11-02

Owner selected white painted MDO instead of aluminum. This is a sheltered screening and
guard infill wall under the canopy, not the house weather enclosure. No east-face WRB,
rainscreen cavity, hat rails, metal returns, clips or separate cassette flashing system.
The west shear plywood, corrugated skin, posts, brace cap and guard framing remain.

## Sheet notes

### General
- White painted 3/4-inch exterior MDO, directly screwed to framing; no east cavity or WRB.
- Panel faces x=77.875 inches; tops +47.5, bottoms +2 inches. Keep 1/8-inch joints open.
- Add five KDAT 2x4 vertical edge nailers; see dimensioned support schedule below.
- No. 8 x 2-1/2-inch stainless screws: 6 inches at vertical edges, 12 inches at field studs.
- North panel: 2.625 x 3.750-inch open-ended garage tie relief; retain bolt/tool access.
- Prime and paint both faces, cut edges and holes; retain upper cap and drainage below.

## Detailed installation schedule

- East infill: nominal 3/4-inch Roseburg DuraGard Premium exterior-grade MDO, white painted,
  screwed directly to the framing. Face x=77.875 inches, 1/4 inch behind timber.
- Four panels, elevations +2 to +47.5 inches. Widths 7.750, 26.9375, 26.9375 and 5.875 inches.
  Maintain the modeled 1/8-inch perimeter and central panel gaps. Do not rigidly fill gaps.
- Support vertical panel edges on existing framing or added KDAT 2x4 nailers at stations
  7.125, 14.375, 40.625, 66.875 and 74.125 inches from the south wall node. Nailers fit
  between existing plates, +0.56 to +45 inches; top plate supports the panel tops.
- Connect each new nailer at each end with three 8d stainless toenails, staggered to avoid
  splitting. Field-treat cuts and holes in treated framing before closing.
- Removable No. 8 x 2-1/2-inch 304 stainless pan-head wood screws with white heads, at
  maximum 6 inches along vertical panel edges and 12 inches on intermediate studs.
  Keep screws at least 3/8 inch from panel edges; pilot drill and do not overdrive.
  Seat heads without crushing the overlay. Reserve 1/8 inch beyond panel face for heads.
- At the north end retain an open-ended 2.625 x 3.750-inch relief, stations
  76.625–79.25 and +22.125 to +25.875 inches, around the existing garage tie block.
  Seal its cut edges. Keep bolt/tool access and straight-out panel removal possible.
- Fill exposed edge voids, ease sharp edges, seal all cut edges and holes. Apply a compatible
  exterior wood primer and two white exterior acrylic topcoats to both faces and edges,
  following the coating manufacturer. Recoat field cuts before installation. Do not use MDF.
- Retain the existing upper cap and open drainage below; avoid ledges and sealant dams that
  hold water against the MDO. Inspect paint and especially cut edges during maintenance.

## Support, loading and purchasing

The MDO is finish/infill, not credited as shear sheathing or as an independent handrail.
Keep the existing structural guard and west shear load path. Maximum intermediate stud
spacing remains 16 inches; the added centre-joint nailer supports both edges. Screws
engage framing rather than relying on plywood edge withdrawal. Panel/support attachment
still needs ordinary wind/infill capacity confirmation using the actual delivered panel
and framing species; a fully engineered fastening capacity is not inferred from paint
or from a generic MDO product description.

Model weight uses 600 kg/m3 plywood, all five new 2x4 nailers and a conservative 2 lb
reserve for dry coating and fasteners. Support solids are coordinated separately but
included once in the effective panel areal density. No beneficial unlisted weight is
credited. Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/entry_screen_mdo_report.py` for the current
house load-path results in `entry_screen_mdo_calculations.json`.

One 48 x 96-inch sheet fits the four panels: place the two 26.9375 x 45.5-inch panels
end-to-end in one strip; the narrow panels fit alongside. Allow saw kerf and factory-edge
cleanup. Cut the north panel's tie relief after checking the actual block. Five nailers
require three 8-foot 2x4s at 44.44 inches each. Confirm local nominal 3/4-inch MDO stock,
actual thickness, white paint sample and framing alignment before cutting. Substrate
flatness, joints and screw heads will be visible; no formed-metal sample is required.

The pricing table is a budget allowance, not a supplier quote. Obtain a local price for
