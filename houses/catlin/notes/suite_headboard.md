# Suite headboard — white painted board-and-batten

Approved 2026-10-20. `WP-S-SUITE-HEADBOARD` covers only W-S-SN1 and W-S-SN2,
about 52.7 SF from finished floor to 72" AFF. It replaces the decorative cherry
shiplap, preserving the 5/8" gypsum beneath it, staggered studs and cavity fiberglass.
No change to the acoustic assembly and no added STC credit.

Use bought 1/4" paint-grade MDF backing, with purchased S4S tulip poplar:

- Eight nominal 1x3 battens/end stiles, actual 3/4" x 2-1/2", cut 65" long.
- One nominal 1x4 top rail, actual 3/4" x 3-1/2", approximately 105-1/2" long.
- Top of rail at 72" AFF. No projecting cap or separate bottom rail.

The current resolved run is 105.48" (finish-face deductions included). Eight stile
centers are equally spaced at about 14.71", including both end stiles; the layout
continues across the SN1/SN2 modeling boundary. Locate MDF joints beneath battens,
not in exposed fields. Order full sheets and stock board lengths from a field-verified
cut layout; takeoff reports backing area with waste and actual trim cut lengths.

The existing 3/4" x 3-1/2" painted baseboard remains the bottom edge. MDF continues
behind it, building its face out to the same 1" projection as the battens. Batten
bottoms butt its top. Scribe end stiles at adjoining finishes and coordinate the
1/4" base offset where the treatment ends; do not add a second base order.

Prime MDF faces and cut edges, fill fastener holes, and use white satin enamel
matching the existing painted trim (#f0ede6 in the model). MDF field preparation
and painting are in the backing rate; poplar preparation/painting in its LF rates.
These are provisional estimates, not supplier quotes.

Attach only within the bedroom-side framing system. Do not connect opposite stud
rows with fastening or blocking; keep the gypsum continuous and seal penetrations.
ED-S-SUITE-RC5 retains its 12-3/4" wall station and 16" AFF mounting elevation in a
clear field bay. Extend its box and faceplate 1/4" to the MDF finish face. Keep the
plate clear of the battens; verify the cutout and extension on site.

Cherry window stools and bedroom-face door casing remain. MDF/poplar are purchased
procurement quantities and never part of the custom sawyer/millwork schedule.
