# Closed-cell spray foam — performance spec, qualifying products, application

Researched 2026-09-16 from the evaluation reports, TDSs and application guides themselves;
the product survey re-read every report on 2026-10-21. Covers every ccSPF line: the truss
wall (4" exterior, `EXT_2X6`), the roof flash (5" under the deck,
`notes/roof_flash_and_batt.md`), the garage bays (2") and the rims (3").

**SELECTED: Amrize (ex-Holcim) Enverge OnePass HFO, IAPMO UES ER-859** (owner, 2026-10-21),
for every line. It is the only product found that meets every requirement in §1 AND lists
WRB in its own report (§3.5), so the wall's water plane needs no alternate approval. It is
NOT the only WRB-listed foam: BASF WALLTITE LWP (ESR-2642 §4.5) is listed too, but fails
item 2 (§2b). The spec still governs: a substitute must meet §1, and any substitute without
item 7 reopens the Minn. R. 1300.0110 request (§2a).

## 1. Requirements

Required:

1. **2 lb HFO closed-cell SPF with a CURRENT evaluation report** (ICC-ES, IAPMO UES or
   Intertek CCRR). A lapsed report does not qualify, whatever the product was.
2. **High lift: ≥ 4" in a single pass by the report** (or its TDS where the report defers
   to it). A 2"/pass product (e.g. Heatlok HFO *Pro*, ESL-1372) forces a cooling wait or a
   second visit on the wall, and the pricing assumes neither.
3. **Air-impermeable** at ≥ 1" — ASTM E2178 (material) / E283. This is what R806.5 item
   5.1.3 reads on the roof, and the wall has no other air barrier.
4. **R316.3 surface burning:** ASTM E84 flame spread ≤ 75 / smoke ≤ 450. Note every report
   below tests at ≤ 4"; **the roof's 5" exceeds the tested thickness**. ER-859 §3.3.2 closes
   it for OnePass: any thickness behind a 1/2" gypsum (R316.4) thermal barrier. Any other
   product: confirm the report's allowance at submittal.
5. **Class II vapour retarder at design thickness** (all qualifiers reach it by 2").
6. **≥ 4" on a vertical surface with no backing**, by the report. The one-tier truss wall
   carries nothing in the foam (`catlin_truss_engineering.md` §1 item 2). OnePass: 7-1/2"
   (ER-859 §3.3.4.2.2). Heatlok High Lift: 7-1/4" (ESR-4073 §4.4.2). SealTite: 7.5"
   (ER-720). OPTIMAXX: confirm at submittal.

Strongly preferred, in this order:

7. **WRB recognition in the evaluation report** (ASTM E331 / ICC-ES AC71). The wall has no
   sheet WRB, so without it the water plane is a Minn. R. 1300.0110 alternate-approval
   request on the maker's own data. A report listing closes that item outright.
8. ABAA Evaluated Material (air and water).
9. NFPA 285. **Not an IRC requirement** (IBC Type I-IV walls only). No report found lists an
   assembly matching this wall — 4" exterior foam, no WRB, metal panel on girts — so it is
   informative only unless the AHJ asks.

### 2a. Reviewed and not qualifying

- **Heatlok HFO Pro** (IAPMO ER-565, rev. 2026-05, to 2027-03) — 2"/pass fails item 2, and
  **no WRB listing**, the 2020 edition included. A reviewer cited ER-565 and ESR-4073 as WRB
  listings (2026-10-21, twice); neither contains R703.2, 1403.2/1404.2, E331 or AC71. ER-565's
  only "WRB" text is in its NFPA 285 assembly tables, where a WRB is a separate component
  that may sit over the foam, not a use of it.
- **BASF WALLTITE LWP**: see §2b. It is WRB-listed but fails item 2.
- **BASF WALLTITE Max** (ESR-2642) — high lift (3.5" back to back), but §4.5's WRB listing
  names LWP only.
- **Covestro EcoBay CC** (ex-Bayseal CC/CCX, ESR-3076) — §4.5 WRB is real in the 2016 report
  (reissued 2016-05, renewal due 2017-18). It is **not current**: ICC-ES's report directory
  returns ESR-3076 (and the older Bayseal ESR-2072) as not found, where the same URL serves
  ESR-2642 and ESR-4073 (checked 2026-10-21). Fails item 1 until a current report is shown.

### 2b. WRB-listed but not qualifying

**BASF WALLTITE LWP** (ICC-ES ESR-2642, reissued 2026-01, renewal 2027-01). The one foam
other than OnePass whose current report lists WRB: §4.5, alternative to IBC 1403.2 / IRC
R703.2 at ≥ 1" continuous, with the joints and penetrations sealed in LWP, under AC377 §3.1.6.
HFO-blown (BASF TDS 2023-11). It fails **item 2**: the report sends max pass to the
installation instructions and requires 10 min/inch cure between passes (§4.2). BASF's guide
gives **2" max per pass**, no dual-pass, so 4" is two passes and a 20 min wait per bay, and
the roof's 5" is three passes. This is the **fallback if OnePass cannot be bought or
installed**: the WRB item stays closed by report, and item 2 is a schedule and labour cost,
not a code problem. WALLTITE HP+, US, US-N and SPRAYTITE 81206 are not named in ESR-2642's
§4.5; confirm any of them in its own current report first.
- **Ambit Ambi-Tite 204 HFO** (ESR-4427) — no WRB in scope.
- **JM Corbond IV** — IAPMO ER-146 lapsed 2026-07-31 (not renewed as of 2026-09-16). Also
  3.5"/pass in the report (4" in the TDS; the report governs). Re-admit on renewal.
- **UPC 2.0 High Lift HFO** (CCRR-0375) — 4"/pass not verified in a report.

## 3. Application

**Weather is a hold, not a preference.** Spray only on a dry day with substrate and ambient
**60-80 F**, sheathing / deck **< 16% MC** on a pin meter, bay by bay. In that window every
qualifier's single-pass limit is ≥ 4".

**Recommended: two back-to-back passes in one visit.** Pass 1 picture-frames every framing
edge, block side, buck and hanger flange (fillet, never butt square — BSI-048) and lays the
field at 1" — never thinner, whatever lower minimum a TDS allows; pass 2 brings the full depth as soon as pass 1's
surface is back under the maker's re-coat temperature (Carlisle: 10 min or ≤ 100 F; Huntsman:
core < 100 F; OnePass: read the Amrize application guide before the spray day, not yet
done). The point is corners: a thin first pass tacked into every inside corner is
more likely to seal them than one thick lift that bridges them.

| Line | Pass 1 | Pass 2 | Single pass allowed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall, 4" | 1" | 3" | yes, where 4" ≤ the product's limit at the day's temperature |
| Roof, 5" (overhead) | **1.5-2"** (full deck adhesion, `roof_flash_and_batt.md` §9) | balance, ≤ the product's pass limit (OnePass 4"; Heatlok 3.25" back to back) | no — 5" exceeds every limit above 70 F |

**No thin flash coat under 1".** Huntsman: "Flash coating to warm the surface is not a
recommended practice … may not have enough exothermic reaction present to properly cure."
JM ER-146 §3.2.3: a pass "of less than 1 inch … on cold surfaces [is] to be avoided and may
result in loss of adhesion of subsequent passes." Picture-framing itself is contractor
practice (BSC GM-2103), not in any maker's guide — it is fine as the first pass, not as a
separate sub-1" coat.

**Never exceed a pass limit to save time.** All three makers name over-thick lifts as a
charring and spontaneous-combustion risk, hours after application.
