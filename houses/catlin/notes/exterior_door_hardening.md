# Exterior swing door hardening — 2026-10-25

Owner scope: D-M-ENTRY, D-M-BALC, D-B-PATIO and D-S-DECK-E. Garage service and
sectional doors are excluded. Product identity, quantities, heights, framing and
finish live on DT-EXT-SWING36 and DT-EXT-FRENCH60 in `plan/door_types.py`; the
hardware sets and installation instructions print on A-601.

## Entry selection

Keep the opaque 36 × 80-inch Therma-Tru Smooth-Star fiberglass inswing leaf,
1¾-inch nominal thickness, composite adjustable sill and composite jambs/head.
Single-point double-bore prep: 2⅜-inch backset, 5½-inch centres. Use a Schlage
Century / Latitude passage lever in matte black 622 at 36 inches and Encode Plus
BE499WB CEN 622 deadbolt at 41½ inches. The DoorScope DS2000 is at 60 inches.
These heights are above the leaf bottom and were confirmed by the owner.

Field-paint black outside and white inside, including the matching frame faces;
finish edges per the door manufacturer's instructions. Ask for a factory-black
alternate price. The earlier multipoint front-entry selection is superseded.

Specify StrikeMaster II Pro reinforcement (Door Armor alternate), and request
Therma-Tru's Security Strike Plate, with dealer cost and compatibility pending.
The prehanger must coordinate the composite frame, reinforcement and factory
strike as one compatible assembly; this is not an instruction to stack plates
that have not been approved together. Specify approved 3-inch hinge/strike screws
with the correct head and gauge; confirm they engage solid framing rather than
merely the composite frame. The factory security strike literature describes
2½-inch screws; StrikeMaster also supplies 2½-inch frame screws. The 3-inch
project selection requires the suppliers’ approval, including any warranty implications.

## Installation and framing

- Solid composite shims directly behind the latch/deadbolt strikes and every
  multipoint keep. Low-expansion window/door foam seals the frame-to-RO gap;
  it does not replace shims or structural fastening.
- Seal the exposed foam core in the DS2000's 2⅜-inch viewer bore and caulk the
  exterior flange. Obtain written confirmation of warranty coverage before drilling.
- Double jack and king studs on each side of all four openings. Full-depth 2x
  blocking in the immediately adjoining clear stud bays at hinge, latch, deadbolt
  and keep anchor heights. Hinge centres at 10/40/70 inches and French keep
  coordination positions at those heights are schematic; the prehanger's actual
  hinge/keep locations must be reflected in the type before fabrication.
- The three French pairs retain Endura PanoLock+, BE365, passage lever and Ultimate
  Flip-Lever Astragal. One multipoint set and one astragal per pair, six hinges.
  PanoLock+, astragal and hinges remain inside the prehung price; takeoff rows
  identify/count them at zero incremental cost.
- Full-depth closed-cell spray foam replaces batts in the first clear jamb bay
  outside each doubled pack. EXT_2X6 deducts fiberglass, GARDEN_FRAMED_2X6 deducts
  mineral wool. The area excludes plates, studs and the new blocking; foam and
  batt deductions share the same derived polygons.

**Entry fit:** the 36-inch opening in the 48-inch W-M-N3 leaves exactly 6 inches
per side. Both doubled packs fit; both kings and both jacks remain on each side.
The full-height kings replace the overlapping finish-backing tee ladder rungs,
without removing structural tee studs. No `frame_end_supports` exception is used.
Doubled packs also require one additional outer-girt support block at each of
W-M-S2 and W-S-S2, keeping the snapped support bays within the existing limit.
There is no foam cavity in those solid packs: the resolver follows the continuous
framing into W-M-N2 and W-M-N3B for the actual adjoining bays and backing.

## Moisture coordination

The exterior foam is interrupted at the returns and buck, which is why these
jamb bays receive ccSPF rather than the usual batt. This applies to D-B-PATIO as
well: its exterior XPS is interrupted at the door in the same way.

Interior cavity ccSPF plus 4-inch exterior ccSPF (or XPS at the basement walkout)
limits drying of the plywood between them. The design rationale is that exterior
insulation keeps that plywood warm; this is a **reasoned project exception**, not
a Glaser pass or a hygrothermal simulation result. Verify the sheathing is dry
before spraying, protect it from rain during construction, and maintain the sill
pan, drainage and weather seals. No check result certifies this sealed local stack.
