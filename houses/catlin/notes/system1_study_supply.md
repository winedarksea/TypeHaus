# Second-storey study: soffit sidewall supply

Owner-approved 2026-10-04. `REG-S-HP-STUDY2` stays at 75 cfm but moves to
SF-S-DUCT's east face, x=21'-5 1/2", y=3'-7 1/2", throwing east across the study.
Its 8x6 face runs from 94 5/8" to 100 5/8" AFF, centred on the soffit leg's
99 1/8" centreline above the storey datum (97 5/8" AFF). The finished floor
is 1 1/2" above that datum; wall mounts are measured from the finished floor.
A drawn 6" round galvanized boot connects directly
to DU-S-HP-SOUTH-RISE; the lining opens locally without cutting a framing rail.
The soffit remains a lined enclosure, not an air plenum.

## Selection basis

Use a double-deflection 8x6 sidewall register with opposed-blade balancing
damper. Titus 300R is the performance basis, not a purchased product:
https://www.titus-hvac.com/file/1010/300.301Rperf_300_350_2017.pdf
(page H17 and performance notes H22).

The published 8x6 row at 78 cfm, 300 fpm core velocity and zero spread lists
5/9/16 ft throw at 150/100/50 fpm terminal velocities. That is near the
75 cfm design point and the room's approximately 14 ft eastward reach.
The existing 12x6 family starts at 123 cfm in this table, so it is not the
selection basis for this terminal. The 6" boot carries 75 cfm at ~382 fpm.

Start with a narrow eastward jet and aim slightly upward toward the ceiling,
which is about 6" above the face's top. Catalogue throw is isothermal;
confirm cooling drop and heating distribution for the submitted grille and
actual supply temperatures, then balance to 75 cfm and adjust vanes on site.
Do not aim the supply back toward D-S-STUDY2 or D-S-PLANT.

## Branch consequences

DU-S-HP-SOUTH-RISE carries 250 cfm before this take-off and 175 cfm after it.
DU-S-HP-SOUTH now carries 175 cfm to the attic desk (100) and plant room (75),
and ends east at its x=19'-6" riser station. Its length is 7'-6", down from
10'-8" after the attic register relocation. The new sidewall boot adds
2'-0" of 6" round duct; the removed 3'-2" was 10x6 rectangular duct.
