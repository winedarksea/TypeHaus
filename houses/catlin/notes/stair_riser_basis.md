# Stair risers — open or closed, and of what (2026-09-28)

Model: `Stair.riser_thickness` / `riser_material` on every stringer flight, and
`MillworkStandard.riser_material_ref` (`plan/millwork.py`) for the oak ones. Generated by
`resolve/stairs/common.py::_riser_member`. Graded by `code.R311_7_5_1_open_risers`
(`checks/code/mn_residential/open_risers.py`). `tests/test_stair_risers.py` reproduces §3–§4.

## 1. Why

No flight resolved a riser, so every one was an open-riser stair. Nothing graded that, and
`takeoff/stairs.py` billed a riser per tread that the model never drew.

## 2. The rule

IRC R311.7.5.1: open risers are allowed where the opening does not pass a 4" sphere, but
only if it is more than 30" above the floor or grade below.

- **Opening** = riser − the board and any covering above it. A nosing overlaps in plan and closes nothing
  vertically.
- **Above 30"** is read at the opening's TOP (conservative), over the flight's springing
  floor.
- **Landing edge**: ST-M2S's landing is 3/4" oak on 3/4" ply (1.5"), but its landing tread's
  lip drops to the 1.75" tread depth over the riser below, so that step's board is 1.75" too.
- **Closed** = a `riser` member stands on that step. A box or cast tier is its own riser.

flight FAILs open.

## 4. Closed

Every flight gets a 3/4" board on each step, face on the riser line, set back under the
nosing. The board at the head of a run, and each winder's board, stands in front of the
framing it faces instead.

| Flight | stock | pieces | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST-M2S | oak-riser, 4/4 S4S, owner-milled | 16 | 3/4 × 5.78 × 42 3/8" |
| ST-S2A | oak-riser | 14 + 2 | 3/4 × 5.75 × 36", two fan risers × 38.42" (√(24² + 30²)) |
| ST-B2M | plywood-subfloor, under carpet | 14 + 1 | 3/4 × 5.86 × 41 1/16", and the split-landing step × 50 7/8" |
| ST-G-SERVICE / ST-SG-PORCH | kdat | 5 + 5 | 3/4 × 5.30 / 5.10 × 36" |

Board height = R − the board and any separate covering above (the riser's top is the
substrate's underside). 5.78" off
4/4 rough is one board well inside MW-STANDARD's 18" supply.
