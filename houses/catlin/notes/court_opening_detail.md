## Sheet notes

### General
- D-B-PATIO and WIN-B-SAUNA: no flashing is modelled at either. Build to these notes.
- Water plane: the 60 mil waterproofing. Opening membranes lap onto it, shingle-fashion.
- Veneer arches are self-supporting, under the porch: no lintel, no head flashing (IRC R703.8.5).
- WIN-B-SAUNA is 3'-8" above the court, clear of the flood line. Liner vapour return seals inside.

### Keyed
- [K1] Buck through the 6" foam to the waterproofing, sealed to it.
- [K2 @ host#layer:waterproofing:out] Head: membrane band on the buck, lapped under the waterproofing above. Jamb bands lap over the sill pan.
- [K3] Sill pan sloped 1/4"/ft, with back dam and end dams 6" up the jambs, lapped over the waterproofing below.
- [K4] D-B-PATIO: the court ponds to the threshold. Pan back and end dams stand 6" above the threshold.
- [K5] D-B-PATIO: frame on sealant at the back dam only; pan front open to drain.
- [K6] D-B-PATIO: door with a NAFS water-penetration rating at the threshold.
- [K7 @ host#layer:eps-ci:out] Veneer cavity: 4" clear air. Keep the base-course weeps open; they drain it after the court drains.

# Notes

Rationale and sources: `opening_flashing_spec.md` §8. This file prints verbatim onto the

its backup opening is the same sequence above the flood line, so K1–K3 cover it.
