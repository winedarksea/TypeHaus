# Living-room coffee-table floor receptacle

`ED-M-LIVING-FLOOR-RC1` is centered under `FURN-M-PUZZLE-COFFEE-TABLE` at
x = 327.9 inches, y = 104 inches in the house frame. It sits between the
`FS-M-EAST` joists at y = 96 and 112 inches, outside the dining radiant-heat zone.
The modeled outer envelope clears framing; verify the actual bay and services before drilling.

Preferred assembly: Arlington FLBR5420 series, a recessed 20A tamper-resistant duplex
floor-box kit. Finish suffix remains to be selected. Arlington's [P-19 catalog sheet](https://www.aifittings.com/media/catalog-pages/P-19.pdf)
gives a 5-inch cutout, 6.625-inch flange diameter and **6.550-inch depth** (dimension B;
dimension C, 3.775 inches, is body width). The catalog envelope is modeled below the
finished floor. The thin flange sits over the LVP; the slotted in-use cover keeps plugs
recessed. Fit the gasketed blank cover when unused and blank the unused low-voltage ports.

Cut through LVP and subfloor, secure to the structural subfloor and seal under the flange
as Arlington directs. Coordinate the floating LVP's movement allowance with its installer;
do not use the box to pin the flooring against its installation requirements. Leave joists,
flanges, blocking and other framing intact. The coffee table can move for cover access.

Supply from the existing **CKT-RC-MAIN: 120V, 20A, AFCI breaker** in `ED-B-PANEL`,
using 12/2 NM-B with equipment ground and the supplied NM connector. This is general
living-room power, covered by the existing circuit's 1,500 VA allowance. Circuit
assignments, panel schedule, takeoff, electrical plan and IFC export include the outlet.
Individual branch-circuit cable routing is not modeled.

The floor-box listing check covers E3905.7 / NEC 314.27(B); the existing AFCI and GFCI
rules evaluate this device's circuit and location. This sitting-area outlet is beyond the
18-inch wall reach under E3901.2.3 / NEC 210.52(A)(3), so it supplements wall outlets.

Second choice: a [Leviton TR pop-up floor box](https://leviton.com/products/residential/pop-up-floor-box-receptacles).
Leviton lists vinyl among its flooring applications and describes a 1/4-inch closed-cover
height. Select a specific model and recheck its cutout, depth and open-cover clearance
before substituting; its envelope is not interchangeable with the recessed Arlington kit.
