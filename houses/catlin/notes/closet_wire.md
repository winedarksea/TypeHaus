stations for 60 inches and four per 84-inch tier, including the ends. Shorten
spacing for heavy loads as the selected system requires. Use matching
hardware for the 16-inch shelf and its separate rod, including down clips
at open ends; a shelf end spaced off a side wall needs its own front support.

Fasten back clips into the listed backing courses or studs. Check front

automatically cover them. Land those screws on studs or suitable backing,

### Cutting and buying

[ClosetMaid's installation guide](https://homedepot.closetmaid.com/en-US/Installation/Pages/how-to-guides.aspx)
explicitly permits cutting wire shelving with bolt cutters or a hacksaw and
closet rods with a pipe cutter (preferred) or hacksaw. Cap exposed wire ends
and the south rod ends. Short stock sizes are unnecessary: use longer
12-inch-deep nickel SuperSlide shelving and matching fixed rods, cut to fit.
For separate 29 1/2-inch extensions, candidates are the
[48 x 12-inch nickel shelf, model 34714](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100658326)
and [48-inch nickel fixed rod, model 32057](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100658327).
The full continuous 54 1/4-inch west shelf needs stock longer than 48 inches.
Do not cut a telescoping rod without instructions for that particular product.

The 29 1/2-inch dimension is the installed extension envelope, **not a
pre-measurement saw cut**. Field-measure after wall finishes and end hardware
are installed, account for caps and connector insertion, and follow the
selected bracket's fitting clearance. ClosetMaid's
[wire shelving specifications](https://design.closetmaid.com/en-us/Documents/Wire%20Shelving%20Specifications%20rev_6_12_English_HQ.pdf)
call for wall-to-wall shelf cuts 1/2 to 1 3/8 inches shorter than the wall
measurement. Apply the actual end condition; a supported butt joint is not
a second side wall. Retain the nickel finish; shelf SKU and stock length are
purchase selections rather than a change to the installed layout.

For new construction, a single west shelf per tier cut from longer stock can
span the full 54 1/4-inch line, preserving the model's two tagged sections as
installation zones. Likewise prefer one straight rod from the corner bar to
the south end instead of recreating the old short west-leg stub.

### Supports and continuity

SuperSlide has a separate rod underneath the shelf, rather than using the
shelf's front lip as the hanging rail. Use the matching corner bar to continue
around the L. At a split shelf seam, use a universal shelf joiner with a front
support adjacent to it; a joiner alone does not carry the shelf. If the rod is
split at that seam, use a compatible internal connector with the connection
resting in a rod support. Support both connections to the corner bar.

The manufacturer specifies back clips every 10–12 inches and front supports
at most 36 inches apart; rod supports are required at the ends and at
24–36-inch intervals. The continuous west run needs intermediate front and
rod support; it cannot span 54 1/4 inches on its ends alone. Use the south
side-wall shelf/rod end bracket, wall clips and appropriate front brackets.
North-end attachment at the remaining PAX must suit its panel substrate;
provide an independent wall bracket where the panel cannot carry the load.

Existing west-wall `BK-M-BA2E2-HIGH` and `-GRAB` courses cover both west
sections, and north-leg clips retain `BK-M-CLN-WIRE-HI` and `-LO`. Confirm
actual screw positions against those courses and studs; choose the prescribed
anchor where a bracket screw falls outside them. The obsolete west PAX rail
backing and its 28-inch OVERSIDAN strip are removed. The remaining two north
strips use 9.5 W, or 11.9 W with the existing 25% allowance, on the 30 W driver.
