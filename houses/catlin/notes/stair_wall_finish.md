# Painted stair wall finish

**Applies to:** `W-M-STRW` (`INT_2X6_BRG_MUDROOM_UPPER_GWB`, a variant of
`INT_2X6_BRG_EXPOSED_PLY`). The basement stair-line segments `W-B-STR2`/`W-B-STR3B` swap the
leaf for 5/8" gypsum, and `W-B-STR`/`W-B-STR3` carry Type X; none of them is paneled.

**The leaf is 1/2-inch MDO, overlay on both faces (MDO 2S), primed and painted white on both
faces** (reviewer, 2026-10-23; it was 5/8-inch paint-grade veneer-core plywood). One sheet does
two jobs: the stair face, and the back of every mudroom coat nook. The resin-fibre overlay takes
paint without the veneer grain telegraphing, and it holds a hook screw. Handrails and other
substantial loads still fasten to studs or dedicated backing.

The studs stay at x=117 1/8 to 122 5/8 inches. The MDO finishes at x=123 1/8 inches before
paint, and `FO-S-STAIR`'s west edge and `ST-M2S`'s start are struck off that face, so a change
of leaf thickness moves both. Seal the cut edges, then prime and paint both faces before hanging,
because the nook face cannot be reached for back-priming once the sheet is up.

At a plywood-to-drywall edge, terminate the gypsum with a finished J or reveal trim and leave a
narrow movement joint against the MDO. Do not tape or feather joint compound across the wood
edge; paintable flexible sealant may close the joint after priming.

**Mudroom face.** The coat nooks are open below 7'-0". Above that, 1/2-inch gypsum runs to the
9'-0" ceiling, primed and painted, its lower edge finished with a J or reveal trim. Three flat
red-oak 8/4 courses span each bay: the bench support course, the 6'-0" shelf and the 7'-0" cap
behind the gypsum edge.

**Hook rails.** Two painted poplar 1x4s (3/4 x 3 1/2 S4S) fit between the studs, flat on the
back of the MDO, with bottoms at 42 and 60 inches above the finished floor
(`BK-M-STRW-HOOK-LOW`/`-HIGH`). Glue and screw them through to the MDO and toe-screw them to the
studs; the hooks screw into the rail. The low rail stops at the 34'-0" stud, because the bay
north of it holds `REG-M-XFER-MUD`'s transfer louver at 42-52 inches. Prime and paint them with
the MDO.
