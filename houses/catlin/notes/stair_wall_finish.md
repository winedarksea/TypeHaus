# Painted stair wall finish

**Applies to:** `INT_2X6_BRG_EXPOSED_PLY` on `W-B-STR2`, `W-B-STR3B`,
`W-M-STRW`, and `W-M-STRW2`; its Type X variants remain as authored.

The stair face is nominal 5/8-inch paint-grade veneer-core hardwood plywood. A smooth
birch face is acceptable, but the face is painted, so clear-finish appearance grade is
unnecessary. The plywood is a finish and general hook backing. Handrails and other
substantial loads fasten to studs or dedicated backing.

The studs stay at x=117 1/8 to 122 5/8 inches on both storeys. The plywood and the
under-stair 5/8-inch Type X leaf finish at x=123 1/4 inches before paint. At every
plywood-to-drywall edge, terminate the gypsum with a finished J or reveal trim and leave
a narrow movement joint against the plywood. Do not tape or feather joint compound
across the wood edge; paintable flexible sealant may close the joint after priming.

Before cutting or installing panels, measure the delivered plywood and adjacent gypsum,
then shim the plywood to the same finished plane. A 19/32-inch panel against true
5/8-inch gypsum needs about 1/32 inch of shim. Seal exposed plywood edges and prime the
face for paint. Confirm the trim and joint with a short field mockup before paneling the
full stair run.
