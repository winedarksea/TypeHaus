`REG-S-HP-STAIR` retains its position (x=218.76″, y=282⅝″), elevation and westward throw,
with 50 cfm through a TRUaire **A220VO 12X06**, including its opposed-blade damper.

The former generic 2 11/16″ neck ended near x=221.95″ while the trunk's west surface is

- **TH-HP-STAIR-BOX-01:** fabricated 26ga galvanized register box, 12″ along the wall,
  6″ high, 4″ installed depth behind the grille back. It includes space for the OBD and
  a centred 4″ round back collar. The box occupies x=219.26–223.26″.
- **DU-S-HP-STAIR:** standard 4″ galvanized snap-lock branch, 50 cfm (approximately
  573 fpm), connecting that collar to `DU-S-HP-SUP`. Installed external gap to the trunk
  is 1.74″; add the required collar engagements when cutting the piece. Its modeled
  centreline starts inside the trunk, following the bedroom side-branch convention.
- Collar centre z=221⅝″ absolute. The trunk is centred at 220⅛″ with 8″ vertical depth;
  the 4″ collar is 1½″ above its centre and fits fully inside its height, leaving ½″
  above the branch opening. No grille movement or trunk enlargement is needed.

Mechanically secure and mastic-seal the box, round stub and trunk collar. Fasten the
register box to the soffit framing at the clear bay, without cutting studs or ladder rails;
the framing check includes the complete 4″ box. Fit the grille to a sealed liner return,
retaining access to the OBD through the removable grille. Set the OBD for 50 cfm and aim
both blade sets west across the landing, away from the return.

`REG-S-HP-STAIR-NECK` carries the box's Product record and parent-register identity.
Its own register type preserves the other bedrooms' smaller damper boxes. The register
allowance includes grille and box once; the branch is billed by its duct length. The

instead of accepting a nearby terminal within the implicit-boot distance.
