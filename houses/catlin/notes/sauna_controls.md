# Sauna controller placement

The mandatory Harvia Xenio kit is **CX170-U1-15**, 240 V single-phase. The
[US manual, 02112021/Y05-0653](https://harviaheater.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Harvia-Xenio-Controller-Manual-for-Canada-and-the-USA-North-America-3.pdf)
§1.2 and figure 6b rate this variant for 10.5 kW with one heater feed. The basic
CX170-U1 allows only 7 kW with one feed.

Both outside-room components attach to the **dry gym face of W-B-CS**:

- **ED-B-SAUNA-CONTROL**: 3.4 × 1 × 4.4 in. touch panel, base 56 in. AFF,
  centre 52 5/8 in. along the wall from its north end. It sits above the existing
  sauna light dimmer, north of D-B-SAUNA's opening and swing.
- **ED-B-SAUNA-POWER**: 10.6 × 3 × 10.6 in. power unit, base 60 in. AFF,
  centre 12 in. from the north end. It sits above the towel bar with its cover
  facing the gym. Surface mount; do not recess this enclosure (§3.2).

CKT-SAUNA feeds the power unit, which switches the heater through ED-B-SAUNA-JB.
The touch panel connects to the power unit by the manufacturer's data cable and
has no separate branch feed. The junction box and controller add no second
10.5 kW load. Controller procurement and installation remain included in the
EQ-T-SAUNA-HEATER package price; the two placed component rows have zero
incremental cost.

This records component placement and circuit membership. Control/sensor cable
routes and the heater's temperature/overheat sensor placement are not modeled;
install those per the heater manual and Xenio §3.3, sealing hot-room penetrations
at the foil air/vapour layer.
