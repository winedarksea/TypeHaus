veining that the former procedural kernel smoothed away. Colour uses sRGB;
grout normals and glaze/roughness maps remain independent of printed veining.

platinum grout at the existing nominal 24 x 48-inch scale. Images ship in
the application bundle for offline use. No transmission or displacement is
inferred from the onyx print.
