# Lifeproof Limestone product photography

Floor swap by the owner for Catlin RM-M-BATH2 and RM-S-BATH1 (replaces Opera Blue).
Product: Lifeproof Limestone 12x24 matte porcelain, LP521224HD1P6:
https://www.homedepot.com/p/308500679
Grout: MAPEI Ultracolor Plus FA #5014 Biscuit (warm tan).

- `lifeproof-limestone-face.jpg`: owner-supplied product swatch, 1000 x 1000 px (JPEG q90 of
  the original PNG). Running bond, 12x24 faces; the Innovation Award badge covers the top-left face.

`three/tilePhotoCatalog.ts` crops five full faces strictly inside the photographed joints
(measured from grout brightness profiles): top right, row 2 centre, row 3 centre, row 4 left and
right. The top-left badge face is excluded. The ten-cell atlas (5 x 2, 512 x 1024 px per cell,
2560 x 2048 total) holds the five faces and their 180-degree rotations, offset so no face sits
beside its own rotation. Long axis lies along plan Y, straight grid, 3/16-inch joint.

Matte: no clearcoat, roughness 0.58. Grout is Biscuit, a shade darker than the face. The
owner's installed-room photo informed colour and sheen only; it was never cropped.
