# Trade audit decisions — 2026-09-12

Reviewer fan-out over every catlin bid-package row (768 rows, 29 chunks, two Sonnet reviewers each: A = the sub receiving the package, B = the GC checking placement), contested rows adjudicated by one Opus pass with web sources. Schema: `docs/trade-audit-schema.json`; machine record: `plans/trade-audit.json` (fed back as prior decisions on the next run).

## What the audit found, in one paragraph

Three structural findings dominated the row-level ones. (1) The `structural_solids` table carries a volume for every resolved solid, and the estimate section that prices it is called `concrete`, so 41 rows across nine packages read as "0.01 cy of backflow preventer" or "cy of duct" — the product was already billed by the foot or the piece in its own section. Those categories are now `MIRRORED_SOLID_CATEGORIES` and never reach a package. (2) Quantities carried the estimate's unit, not the sub's: wood beams by the yard, brick by the yard, PV by the watt, trusses by the foot, aggregate by the layer's net area. `bid_package._shaped_quantity` now restates each in the unit it is bought in (bf, SF, ea, cy) and keeps the takeoff figure in the detail column. (3) Every loose piece of furniture was on the cabinet sub's package because `domain == furniture` filed as millwork; the rule now reads the type tag and name.
