Here is a comprehensive list of the most common pieces of furniture found in US homes, organized by room, with their standard sizes and typical ranges.

##  Living Room

    Standard Sofa (3-Seater)

        Average Size: 84" W × 35" D × 34" H

        Typical Range: 72"–90" W × 32"–40" D

        Note: Sectionals vary wildly, but a standard L-shape is often 110" × 85" overall.

    Loveseat (2-Seater)

        Average Size: 60" W × 35" D × 34" H

        Typical Range: 52"–72" W × 32"–40" D

    Armchair / Accent Chair

        Average Size: 35" W × 35" D × 35" H

        Typical Range: 30"–40" W × 30"–38" D

    Coffee Table

        Average Size: 48" W × 24" D × 18" H

        Typical Range: 36"–54" W × 18"–30" D

    TV Stand / Media Console

        Average Size: 60" W × 18" D × 24" H

        Typical Range: 42"–80" W × 15"–20" D

    Side / End Table

        Average Size: 22" W × 22" D × 24" H

        Typical Range: 18"–26" W × 18"–26" D

    65" tv (with stand)
        Average Size: 56.50 * 33.90 * 14.50 inches

98" tv (with stand): 85.30 * 50.10 * 16.50 inches

## Bedroom

    Queen Bed (Most common for primary & guest rooms)

        Average Frame Size: 64" W × 84" D (Mattress is 60"×80")

    King Bed

        Average Frame Size: 80" W × 84" D (Mattress is 76"×80")

    Twin Bed (Common for kids' rooms)

        Average Frame Size: 42" W × 79" D (Mattress is 38"×75")

    Standard Dresser (Horizontal)

        Average Size: 60" W × 20" D × 36" H

        Typical Range: 54"–72" W × 18"–22" D

    Chest of Drawers (Tall / Vertical)

        Average Size: 34" W × 19" D × 50" H

        Typical Range: 30"–38" W × 18"–20" D

    Nightstand

        Average Size: 24" W × 16" D × 26" H

        Typical Range: 20"–30" W × 15"–18" D

## Dining Room

For floor planning, the table is important, but leaving room to pull out the chairs is the most critical metric.

    Dining Table (Rectangular, 6-Seater)

        Average Size: 72" W × 36" D × 30" H

        Typical Range: 60"–84" W × 36"–42" D

    Dining Table (Round, 4-Seater)

        Average Size: 48" Diameter × 30" H

        Typical Range: 42"–54" Diameter

    Dining Chair

        Average Size: 20" W × 22" D × 38" H (Seat height is almost always 18")

## Home Office

    Writing / Computer Desk

        Average Size: 48" W × 24" D × 30" H

        Typical Range: 40"–60" W × 20"–30" D

    Office Chair

        Average Size: 26" W × 26" D (This footprint accounts for the swivel base/casters)

    Bookcase

        Average Size: 32" W × 12" D × 72" H
