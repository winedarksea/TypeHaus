## Escalated

**E1. I1-001: north run 1.319" build-up under the stone is unspecified and unpriced.** North
bases sit on 3 1/2" legs, which is IKEA leg 905.560.71's *minimum* (range 3 1/2–5 1/8"; IKEA US
page). Frame top 33.5, stone underside 34.819. The ladder note, the engine `sektion.py` docstring
and catlin CLAUDE.md all say "≈1 5/16 substrate". K1 says "no extra filler substrate under the
quartz". Only the peninsula sub-top is priced. *Options*: (a) **recommended**: use the peninsula
stack: 4" legs + 3/4" ply sub-top + 1/16 setting (34.8125, inside 1/100"). Price a north/corner
sub-top allowance and restate K1. (b) Legs at ~4 3/4" with the stone direct on the frames, which
leaves a 1/4" toe-kick reveal (FÖRBÄTTRA 4 1/2"). (c) Keep 3 1/2" and name the 1.319 shim/ply.
*Blast*: notes K1/ladder, prices.toml, CLAUDE.md "Support height 3 1/2". No geometry (base types
are 36" envelopes). No checks. *Size* S.

**E2. I1-002: Frigidaire columns lack the published clearances.** Probe: rear 1/8" (fridge x
219.375 vs wall 219.25), freezer end 0 to PANTRYC, fridge end 1/8. The 65 3/4" bay is under the
66 1/4" paired cutout. Install guide FPRU19F8WF (ManualsLib copy): sides 3/16, top and rear 1".
The pair kit's effect on the side gap is unconfirmed. *Options*: (a) pull both units 7/8"
forward (fronts 3 7/8" proud of the 24" cabinets) and confirm the end gaps with the pairing-kit
sheet. (b) **recommended**: first read Frigidaire's "Pairing Kit" doc (frigidaire.ca support
page). If it waives the ends, only the rear moves. (c) Widen the bay ½" by trimming the 2 7/8
cold-upper scribes, which moves PANTRYC. *Blast*: APPL-M-FRIDGE/FREEZER, aisle to the
peninsula, A-102, possibly PANTRYC/K7 scribes. *Size* S–M.

**E5. Floor finish stacks: I4-001, I4-004, I4-005.** Carpet: modelled 3/4". The published
cushion 7/16 + pile 0.56 alone = 0.998, before backing and the 1/4 ply companion. Already
recorded as provisional in `notes/bedroom_carpet.md`, but nothing on the set says so. Tile:
each stack is forced to 0.75 with 0.10–0.15" of total mortar under large-format tile. DITRA-HEAT
is 5.5 mm / 1/4" (Schluter estimator; the 0.25 nominal is fine). SurePly "1/4" is nominal only
on the datasheet (Patriot Timber/Lowe's PDF); the typical actual is about 0.20". *Options*: (a)
**recommended**: one reconciliation pass that sets carpet ≥1.0" (or picks 3/8 cushion + ≤0.4
pile), gives tile mortar ≥3/16 over the membrane, and takes SurePly from a measured sheet. Then
re-strike doorway steps and the D-M-BED2 / D-S-PLANT reducers together (`floor_heights.md`). (b)
Keep the allowances and print them as "provisional — field verify" on A-603. *Blast*:
finished-floor elevations of 6+ rooms, doorway steps, R311 thresholds, door undercuts, base
seats, A-603. *Size* M.
