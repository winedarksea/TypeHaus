## Escalated

### E1-002 — garage and canopy seams laid out per roof (11.56" out of register)
- **Evidence:** `seam_stations`: canopy 450.63..514.63, garage 526.19..814.19 (16" pitch),
  so a 3.97" and an 8.44" pan meet at y≈518 on what `houses/catlin/CLAUDE.md` calls ONE
  plane. Real panels (≈14 ft, under the 30 ft max) run straight across. Model/viewer only;
  the set draws no seams.
- **Options:** (a) derive a shared seam datum for coplanar roofs that abut
  (`abutted_run_keys` already knows the pair) and use it in `panel_stations`,
  `_roof_plane_mesh`'s anchor and `roof_image_ii_pan_end`'s anchor — **recommended**;
  (b) author a datum override on `RF-BW-CANOPY`; (c) accept.
- **Blast radius:** RF-GARAGE/RF-BW-CANOPY panel meshes, pan-end/seam-end hems, GLB/IFC,
  elevation golden hashes for those roofs; no check, no engineered item. **Size S-M.**

### E1-007 — 4" round leader is not a Metal Sales part
- **Evidence:** PBR guide pp. 13-14 stock only 4 x 3-1/2 (58094XX/58097XX) and 4 x 6
  rectangular downspouts; the takeoff bills "Downspout, 4" — Metal Sales 24 ga PVDF".
- **Options:** (a) re-state as 4 x 3-1/2 rectangular (14.0 in² vs 12.57) with 58102XX
  elbows, 58110XX straps and a rect-to-round adapter into the 4" PVC — **recommended**, it is
  stocked and has more capacity; (b) keep round and name it custom-formed / another maker;
  (c) keep as is (misdescribed).
- **Blast radius:** `params/roof_trim.py` `_LEADER_DIA_IN`, `Downspout` geometry (rectangular
  section may need engine support), drainage takeoff row, `box_gutter_sizing.md` outlet
  sizing, elevations. Product swap → owner. **Size M.**

### E1-008 — garage vented ridge has no part number; Metal Sales publishes one
- **Evidence:** RDM 5/2024 p. 41 "Image II Vented Peak": Residential Peak 52134XX over Profile
  Vent 6441899 (16" Image II, 100 ft) with clips 6540000. The catalog already holds
  `IMAGE_II_RESIDENTIAL_PEAK` (unreferenced). `integrity.cladding_trim_coverage` is UNKNOWN on
  exactly this, and CLAUDE.md lists the 13" step cover part number as an open Metal Sales item.
- **Options:** (a) swap to the published Vented Peak — closes the part-number UNKNOWN —
  **recommended**; (b) keep the PIM step ridge + Cobra and wait on Metal Sales.
  Either way the RDM prints **no NFA** for Profile Vent, so `code.R806_2_attic_ventilation`
  stays UNKNOWN until a cut sheet is obtained.
- **Blast radius:** `GARAGE_ROOF` roofing material / ridge trim, `roof_ridge_trim.py`,
  `takeoff/cladding_trim.py` ridge branch, `prices.toml [cladding_trim]`, garage ridge
  geometry. Product swap. **Size M.**

### E1-009 — ridge caps step 1.47" at the canopy/garage joint; no ridge end caps billed
- **Evidence (probe):** canopy closed cap z 141.58-144.75, garage vented cap z 143.22-146.22,
  overlapping 0.88" at y 517.75-518.63; no 52111XX End Cap row anywhere (house gable ends too).
- **Options:** (a) run one cap type across both roofs (follows from E1-008/E1-002's shared
  plane) — **recommended**, decide with E1-008; (b) stop the vented cap short with an End Cap
  and Z-closure at the joint; (c) bill 52111XX end caps at every open ridge end regardless.
- **Blast radius:** ridge members on RF-GARAGE/RF-BW-CANOPY, cladding_trim takeoff, GLB.
  **Size M.**

### E2-001 — BBD75 band: lower panel's top 9.25" past its last girt; lap check reads the trim leg
- **Evidence (probe, W-S-N2):** girts 216-219.5 then the band course 228-231.5; the lower panel
  stops at 227 (`BAND_PANEL_GAP_IN` = 1", decision #99's outlet slot), so its top has no girt
  behind it — 7.5" past the last girt's top edge. BBD75 p. 40 shows "PANEL FASTENER (B), AT
  NAIL STRIP" at the lower panel's top, under the Transition Trim; that screw has no substrate
  here. Walls with an extra course at 224.5-228 (e.g. W-S-S1) are fine. `_band_laps` reports
  "lap the lower panel 2-1/2 in", which is the trim leg below the joint; the leg actually laps
  the panel 1.5" (leg bottom 225.41, panel top 227). The guide gives the leg (2-1/2") but no
  minimum lap on the panel.
- **Options:** (a) drop the band girt course ~1-1/2" so it backs the panel's top 1" and the trim
  shelf both (the vent gap is behind the girts, so the slot stays open) — **recommended**;
  (b) add a short course under the band on walls without one; (c) accept, with the tail held only
  by the trim's pop rivets at 12", and say so in `notes/metal_trim_kit.md`. Separately, measure
  `_band_laps` as leg-over-panel — but at 1.5" against the current 2-1/2" threshold that turns
  the check **FAIL**, so the threshold needs a cited value first.
- **Blast radius:** girt course layout on every N/S BBD wall segment (`board_batten.py`,
  `rainscreen_strips.py`), `structural.girt_course_spacing`, `integrity.rainscreen_cavity`,
  `integrity.cladding_trim_fit`, `girt_screw/W-A-N1` fingerprint if its tributary moves, A-201/202
  elevations, section goldens through the band. **Size M.**
