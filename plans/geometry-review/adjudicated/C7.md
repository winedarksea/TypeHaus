# C7 — Construction set and viewer

Findings: D1 (12), D2 (15), U1 (4); the C7 handoffs from C1–C6 (the ~60 sheet items); MEP drawing
items M1-002, M1-009, M2-001, M2-002 and M3-008. Baseline `247a13e9`, 2 FAIL (both
`code.R311_7_1_stair_width` on ST-S2A). Ranked by "what misprints that a trade builds from".
Review copies of every touched sheet were rendered ungated into the scratchpad (`snap_sheets.py`,
written from `snap_print.py`) and read page by page. `haus print` was not used.

**Two root causes cover 23 findings.**

- **D2-001.** Paper-space nodes were drawn on the model axes. A derived detail card's title
  block and material legend are `space="paper"` Text nodes in the card's own inches.
  `compose_sheet` handed them to `_render_nodes` on the model axes. Wherever the crop held
  (0.5", 1.5..7.8"), they piled up under the drawing. Fix: the sheet composer keeps model
  nodes only, because the permit sheet has its own title block. This closes O5-003, F2-008,
  F5-012 (the pile), E2-003 (the pile), I4-009, O1-006 (the A-530 header) and O3-011 (the
  A-512/515 part).
- **D2-002.** One notes file printed on every condition its pattern spawns. `## Sheet notes`
  bullets may now carry a leading `{glob,!glob}` scope (fnmatch on the derived condition key).
  `details._sheet_note_set` keeps only the matching ones, and the budgets are checked per
  scope. The eave and foundation note files are scoped with it. This closes F5-004, E2-004,
  F4-007 (notes), the A-595..597 eave K-notes and the A-592 notes.
