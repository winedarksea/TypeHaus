### E1 — ST-S2A: RL-A-STAIR's posts stand in the flight and on the head landing (S1-001, S3-001, S3-002, S1-002)

**Resolved 2026-10-31 (option A):** RL-A-STAIR surface-mounted at y 67 1/8"; ST-S2A 36.00" / 32.55"; `stair_head_landing` now sees guard posts.

**Problem and evidence.** The FAILs are real and the check is right. RL-A-STAIR's path is
the well edge (y 69.625"); `fascia_face_offset=0` + `fascia_post_offset` 1.518" puts the
3" posts at y 69.64..72.64, z 240..282. The flight's tread ends are y 69.75..105.75. POST1/2
(x 351-354, 302.9-305.9) reach the 34"-80" envelope over treads 002/007, so the clear width
is 36 − 2.89 = **33.11"** (36" req.), and past RL-A-HANDRAIL's 3.45" projection **29.66"**
(31.5" req.). Handrail alone leaves 32.55", so the handrail is fine and must not move (its
wall clearance is 1.55").

S1-002 is true too. `code.R311_7_6_stair_head_landing` samples floor surfaces and tests walls
only, never solids. POST3 (x 254.88..257.88) is wholly inside the 36" landing rectangle past
the top nosing (x 258.13): **8.68 sq in, 2.89" deep across the landing's south edge**. The
check prints "36 x 36.00 clear" anyway. Making it see guard posts (handrails excluded:
RL-A-HANDRAIL-RAIL1 also sits in the rectangle, as R311.7.1 permits) would **add a FAIL on
ST-S2A**. So it lands with the remount, not before. The same probe finds RL-S-STAIRHEAD-POST2
0.44" into ST-M2S's rectangle, but that guard states no face (E3), so the post isn't drawn
where it really stands.

Also model-only, and moot under option A: RL-A-STAIR states no `fascia_bracket_drop`, so no
bracket is drawn and the posts stop at the joist top (240") instead of reaching a bracket at
~234.5..239.5". The 3.29" bracket hat would make the real intrusion ~3.17", not 2.89", and
`stair_width` reads only the post.

No dated CLAUDE.md or DESIGN-LOG entry picks a fix. `plans/TODO.md` (2026-10-31) lists the
options as "owner's pick". `notes/winder_stair_basis.md` still describes RL-A-STAIR as
"42 inches, on the deck", which suggests surface-mount was the original intent.

**Options.**
- **A (recommended): surface-mount RL-A-STAIR on the deck.** Use the Williams 42" surface
  post kit (Menards 1717824, already named in `RAILING-INT-STAIR-GUARD`'s source), with the
  base's north edge on the well edge. That puts the post centre ~3" south of y 69.625 (posts
  ~y 65.6..68.6). Clears both R311.7.1 FAILs (36.00" between posts, 32.55" past the rail)
  and the head landing. Same product, same height. The base lands mostly on the 3.5"
  trimmer pack and needs blocking for the 4 GRK RSS screws (ESR-3485 wood row, SG ≥ 0.50).
  Land the `stair_head_landing` post-awareness and the `stair_width` bracket read in the
  same change.
- **B: stop RL-A-STAIR at the 25'-5 1/8" newel and let RL-A-FLIGHT-GUARD guard the deck edge
  west of it** (TODO's second option). Its posts are already outside the flight, but at
  y 66.73..69.73 they run into the trimmer pack (z 228..240) that they would have to pass to
  reach deck level. That needs a notch or a new junction detail. Not recommended.
- **C: widen ST-S2A / FO-A-STAIR by ≥ 3.3".** North moves W-S-SS2 / W-A-SN (the CLAUDE.md
  W-S-SS2 note: 35-1/2" already), the study partition, the bookcase pocket and the handrail.
  South moves the trimmer pack, RL-A-FLIGHT-GUARD and the Study 2 window seat. Size L.

### E4 — Guard rails drawn off the panel, not the post (S3-004)

**Problem.** `resolve/railings/frame.py` centres the top and bottom rails on z0 and z0+h.
RL-A-STAIR draws RAIL1 at z 281.25..282.75 (0.75" over the post top) and RAIL2 at
239.25..240.75: the bottom rail straddles the joist datum, 0.75-2.25" *below* the finished
oak at 241.5. Williams CP262406B is a 39" panel on a 42" post (Menards 1717891), so the real
top rail sits at or under the post top and the bottom rail a few inches above the walking
surface. Relatedly, `base_elevation=ft(20)` is the joist datum, so the drawn "42"" guard is
~41.25" above the finished floor. That is code-neutral (≥ 36") and model-only.

**Options.**
- **A:** add the panel height and bottom gap to `RailingType` and place the rails off them,
  for every guard house-wide.
- **B:** leave it as is (model-only, no sheet value).

**Recommendation:** A, low priority.

**Blast radius.** Every guard's rail solids; the R312.1.3 bottom-gap reading; elevation,
section and GLB goldens.

**Size:** M.
