p. 2 (broan-nutone.com Spec_Sheet_B210E75RT_En_2.pdf): ports 1/2 are 4-5/16" and 4-3/16" from

and EA in **one column** 12" apart, and every start is 5–16" off a collar in either
handedness (confirmed). The wrapped OA/EA first legs sit about 1" into the case top and the
two 12" wraps touch (M1-004, follows from the same starts). *Options:* (a) **recommended**:
author the Broan port pattern plus a rotation on EQ-T-BROAN-B210E75RT. Put the box west (the
east face is 0.06" off EQ-B-WH). Re-route the four legs off the real collars with 6→8
increasers above the case on a branch (`route-run` skill). (b) Keep the schematic starts and
print "connect to unit collars per manufacturer" on M-101. *Blast:* EQ-B-ERV,
DU-B-ERV-SUP/RET-TRUNK, DU-ERV-OA/EA (+ wraps), `erv_static_budget` (currently UNKNOWN 0.441"),
M-101, elevation golden. *Size* L (routing).

**E2. M1-003 (+ M1-008, M2-007): legs shorter than their elbow take-offs.** Re-probed: 39 of

DU-B-ERV-R-GYM's 18.2°/95.3° turns are already UNKNOWN. *Options:* (a) **recommended**: add a
laying-length term to `mep.fitting_pattern` that reports UNKNOWN, not FAIL, until stocked
stamped-elbow radii are sourced. Then re-route the worst legs on a branch. (b) Author offset
fittings at the short legs. *Blast:* about 9 ducts, `erv_static_budget` (it assumes r/D 1.5),
M-10x. *Size* L.

IECC R403.5.3's R-3. *Blast:* `run_through_stud` (+2 FAIL under (b)), PR-B-HW-WASH. *Size* S
(check) + M (route).
