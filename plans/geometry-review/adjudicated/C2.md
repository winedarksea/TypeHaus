## Escalated

### E1: O4-002, how the buck-lined interior reveal of an outie window is finished (S–M)
**Problem.** The frame is at mount −6.0" with its inner face at −2.34" (Drutex) or −2.75"
(Andersen). The gypsum return now stops at the sheathing (0.0"). On every one of the 38
metal-wall windows this leaves a 2.34–2.75" band at the jamb and head that is lined with the
3/8" buck and membrane but carries no specified finish. A board return cannot simply run
on, because the buck steps the jamb in 3/8"+ at the sheathing plane. The stool already
covers the sill. `notes/window_and_door_procurement.md` §6 says only that the reveal is
"deep".
**Options.**
- (a, recommended) Treat the foam-zone reveal as a jamb extension: a primed/PVC 3/4" board
  from the frame to the sheathing plane, with the return butting it. It would be a
  `WindowStool`-like derived record, billed by LF.
- (b) Fur the stud-zone jamb out to the buck line so one gypsum return runs to the frame.
  That needs a bead at the frame and moves the board width by 3/8".
- (c) Order the units with factory extension jambs (Andersen sells 4-9/16"/7-1/8" kits). Drutex
  would need a supplier answer (§2.6).

**Blast radius.** `window_returns.py`, takeoff `[drywall]`/`[drywall_trims]`, A-512/A-513
details, section goldens (`center_section`, `detail_opening_perimeter-EXT_2X6*`), and the viewer.

### E2: O2-003, MDF 1x4 casing is 11/16", not 3/4" (S)
Retail primed MDF 1x4 is 11/16" (0.669", Home Depot 242738) or 21/32" (Lowe's ReliaBilt);
3/4" MDF is a special order. `TrimStandard.thickness` is one value shared by casing and base
(pine FJ / PVC, which really are 3/4"). Modelling MDF at 11/16" therefore makes the base
1/16" proud of the casing at every leg, which is the wrong way round for trim. This is not
a one-row fix.
- (a, recommended) Keep 3/4" and change the casing stock to primed FJ pine 1x4 (it is
  already the base stock). This is a product swap and needs the owner.
- (b) Add a per-material thickness and accept, or back-bevel, the proud base.
- (c) Specify 3/4" MDF from a mill.

Blast radius: `products_trim_stock.py`, `materials.py:332`, TrimStandard, 46 casings, millwork
takeoff.

### E3: O1-003, bypass/bifold ROs come from the swing-door equation (M)
D-M-MUDC (48" bypass): RO 50.236", basis "derived: 1" jamb allowance (generic: no sourced
track)". O-S-CLOSET (56" bifold): 58.236". Retail 48×80 bypass sets are sized to the
finished opening (≈46–48" RO). Options: (a, recommended) give SLIDE/bypass its own rule,
RO = nominal width plus a head-track allowance, and re-check the corner-pack clearance at
`main.py:1114`; (b) author `rough_opening` on a house bypass type with a sourced product.
Blast radius: RO width, header and jambs (C3 framing), A-601, and checks reading the RO.

### E8: O3-003, the exterior-door frame anchor ignores the sheathing (S–M)

jamb would stand 0.44" proud of the gypsum. Recommended: anchor at the sheathing outer
face when a factory depth is authored, then author 6-9/16 on the service door. The sill
pan back moves with `frame_inner_m`.
