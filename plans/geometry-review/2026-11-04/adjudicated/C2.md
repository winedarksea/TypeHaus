## Escalated

**E1 — Centreline strip footings are narrower than IRC Table R403.1(1) and over the printed soil bearing (F5-101, new; top escalation).**
- **The table row.** IRC 2018 Table R403.1(1), 50 psf snow band, light frame, 1,500 psf soil:
  - 2 story—plus basement: 25×7.
  - 3 story—plus basement: 28×9.
  - At 2,000 psf, the same two rows are 19×6 and 21×6.
  - Footnote b: "Based on 32-foot-wide house with load-bearing center wall … for every 2 feet of adjustment … add or subtract 2 inches of footing width and 1 inch of footing thickness". At 36' that is +4" and +2", which gives:
    - 1,500 psf: 29×9 (2 story) and 32×11 (3 story).
    - 2,000 psf: 23×8 and 25×8.
- **The model.** FT-B-CS/CS2/CN/CN2 are 20×8.
- **Hand bearing.** The house's own basis (`notes/main_floor_truss_basis.md` §5) is D+L 3,205 plf. Divided by 1.667 ft that is 1,923 psf; with the strip's own weight (167 plf) it is 2,023 psf.
- **Soil values do not agree.** S-001 prints 1,500 psf, the MN profile default. `plan/site.py` declares GM, which `engineering/soil.py` maps to IBC 1806.2 class 4 at 2,000 psf.
- **Verdict.** The strips fail the prescriptive row under every reading of the story count and either soil value. They are also at or over 2,000 psf by hand. No check grades strip width against soil.
- **Options:**
  - (a) **Recommended:** widen the four centreline strips to the table width for the reading the AHJ accepts. That is 32" (×11") at 1,500 psf, 3-story, which is the reading S-001 prints. Also add a strip-width read (Table R403.1(1) as a typed row with footnote b) to `bearing_wall_footing`, and re-check the perimeter strips against R403.1(3) for the concrete walls.
  - (b) Hold 20" on a stated net-bearing calc at a measured allowable (geotech report).
  - (c) EOR sign-off.
- **Blast radius:** FT-B-CS/CS2/CN/CN2 solids, the S-100 footing schedule (a new row), rebar backout, `bearing_wall_footing` projections (P ≤ T at 8" thickness: 32" fails P ≤ T unless T rises to 11"), the frost/bed solids under them, the section goldens through x=18', and the concrete takeoff.
