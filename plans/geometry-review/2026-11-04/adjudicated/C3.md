## Escalated

**I1-101 — Fridge column hinge against W-M-PAN-S (new, owner-visible).** The fridge stands 5/16"
off the partition (probe). The Frigidaire Resource Guide gives 35.82" wide at 90° (3.2" past the
cabinet side) and 130° max, and says to allow for the door beside a wall. The doors reverse only
with TTDRRVFRKIT(2), and the model has no handing. *Options*: (a) **recommended**: hinge the north
(fridge) door on its south side, at the pair seam, so it swings away from the wall. Order the
reversal kit and author the handing. (b) Keep the wall-side hinge and confirm a 90° stop clears the
handle. (c) Widen the north gap ~3", which moves PANTRYC and the bay. *Blast*: APPL-M-FRIDGE type
(handing field), install parts/prices. Option (c) moves the 66 1/4 bay and K7 scribes. *Size* S.

**I2-105 — BATH2 shower backer (carried, TODO).** Decided on evidence: Swan's older wall manual
(Form #522) allows panels over sheetrock/greenboard, so gwb-mr is not a product misfit. The house's
dated §6 (2026-10-12) asks for more: glass-mat or cement backer under a bonded liquid membrane, and
the model and A-603 do not carry that. *Options*: (a) **recommended**: keep §6 and apply a
glass-mat room-side lining to the two closed shower walls only (a `WallLiningException`, about
48 SF), plus one A-002 spec line. (b) Accept gwb-mr + membrane per Swan and amend §6. *Blast*:
W-M-BA2E2 / W-M-BDN1 lining (main.py: C2's walls), sheet goods, A-603. *Size* S.
