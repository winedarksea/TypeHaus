## Article 690: solar PV systems

**690.1 / 690.2 Scope and listing.** *(summary kept local)*
- **Engine:** not graded; the listing flag is graded for the battery only (R327).

**690.4 General.** (A) PV may supply a building alongside other supplies. (B) Qualified
persons install it. (C) Several PV systems may serve a building; where remote from one another
a 705.10 directory goes at each PV disconnect. **(D) No PV equipment or disconnecting means in
a bathroom.** (E) A power converter may sit where not readily accessible; its disconnect follows
690.15. (F) Floating arrays. (G) Calculations may round to the nearest whole number.
- **Engine:** `code.NEC_690_4_pv_bathroom` (D). Others not graded.

**690.6 ac modules.** Source-circuit rules do not apply; an ac module's output is an inverter
output circuit. Catlin has none.

**690.7 Maximum dc voltage.** The highest voltage between any two conductors, or a conductor
and ground: (1) at most 1000 V for arrays on or attached to buildings and dc circuits inside
them; **(2) at most 600 V on or in a one- or two-family dwelling**; over 1000 V goes to
690.31(G). **(A)** A source circuit's maximum is the series modules' rated Voc summed and
corrected to the lowest expected ambient, by (1) the listed temperature coefficient, (2) Table
690.7(A) (a factor from 1.02 at 24 to 20 degC up to 1.25 at -36 to -40 degC, multiplied on rated
Voc; silicon modules only) or (3) a PE-stamped design (the note offers the ASHRAE extreme annual
minimum as a source). (B) A dc-to-dc converter circuit uses the converter's listed maximum
output, or the sum of series converters. (C) Bipolar. **(D)** A permanent, readily visible label
states the maximum dc voltage (rounding up allowed), at the dc PV disconnect, the conversion
equipment or the distribution equipment.
- **Engine:** `code.NEC_690_7_max_voltage` grades (2) with the (A)(1) coefficient (authored
  `voc_cold`). Table 690.7(A) is coded in `model/pv_voltage.py`, pinned, unused by a check.
  The (D) label is derived at the inverter a PV dc raceway reaches (384 V).

**690.12 Rapid shutdown.** PV circuits on or in buildings carry a shutdown function that cuts
the shock hazard for firefighters, meeting (A)-(D). Exception 1: a ground-mounted array whose
circuits enter a building only to house PV equipment. Exception 2: equipment on unenclosed
detached structures (carports, shade structures, trellises).
- **(A) Controlled conductors:** (1) the PV dc circuits, (2) inverter output circuits from
  inverters inside the array boundary. Exception: circuits from arrays not attached to
  buildings that end on a building's exterior, and 230.6 circuits, are not controlled.
- **(B) Limits.** The boundary is 1 ft from the array in every direction. **(1)** Outside it,
  or more than 3 ft from where the conductors enter a building, controlled conductors are at
  30 V or less within 30 s, between any two conductors or one and ground. **(2)** Inside the
  boundary, either a listed or field-labelled PVHCS (UL 3741), including its initiation device
  if it needs one, or equipment that holds the highest voltage between any two conductors, or a
  conductor and ground, to 80 V within 30 s (a module with its own shutdown device, whose
  maximum voltage comes from 690.7).
- **Engine:** `code.NEC_690_12_rapid_shutdown` grades (B)(2): a module with `rsd`, or a group
  of adjacent modules whose summed cold Voc is at most 80 V. Catlin: 18 modules, every one with
  a receiver, 42.6 V cold each: PASS. Not graded: (B)(1), the PVHCS route, (A)(2) (the inverter
  is in the basement, outside the array boundary, so its output is not controlled).
- **(C) Initiation devices.** (1)(a) Readily accessible, and one or more of: the service
  disconnect, the PV system disconnect, or a listed switch, as the shutdown equipment's
  instructions say. **(1)(b) At a one- or two-family dwelling, the device goes at an outdoor
  location.** (2) "Off" shuts down every PV system tied to the device; other manual or
  automatic initiators are permitted; for one system any single device initiates it. (3) With
  several systems on one service, at most six devices grouped together shut down all of them.
- **Engine:** `code.NEC_690_12_initiation` grades (C)(1)(b): the `rsd_initiator` device lies
  in no room. No such device with PV present is UNKNOWN; indoors is a FAIL; no PV, or no
  dwelling among the project's buildings, is N/A. (C)(1)(a), (2) and (3) are not graded.
- **(D) Buildings with rapid shutdown.** A permanent label at each service equipment where the
  PV connects, or an approved visible spot, shows where the initiation devices are, with a
  simple building-and-roof diagram and the words "SOLAR PV SYSTEM EQUIPPED WITH RAPID SHUTDOWN.
  TURN RAPID SHUTDOWN SWITCH TO THE "OFF" POSITION TO SHUT DOWN PV SYSTEM AND REDUCE SHOCK
  HAZARD IN ARRAY." The heading words are capitals at least 3/8 in. high, all text legible
  against its ground; the figure shows reflective white-on-red. (D)(1): a building with more
  than one shutdown type, or PV with none, gets a plan-view roof diagram with a dotted line
  round what stays energized. (D)(2): the switch carries, on it or within 3 ft: "RAPID
  SHUTDOWN SWITCH FOR SOLAR PV SYSTEM". Catlin has one shutdown type, so (D)(1) is not needed.
- **Engine:** derived: building label at the service root, switch label at each
  `rsd_initiator`; not graded.

raceway.** (A)(3) 105 and 125 degC conductors may use Table 690.31(A)(3)(1) (18 to 4/0 AWG, for

**705.30 Overcurrent protection.** (A) Source output conductors and equipment have OCPDs; a
circuit fed from several sources is protected from each. **(B) OCPDs are at least 125% of
705.28(A)** and may be set per 240.4(B)/(C) (100% for listed continuous-duty assemblies). (C)
Equipment holding OCPDs fed from interconnected sources is marked to show all sources. **(D)
Backfeed suitability:** (1) fused disconnects are suitable unless marked otherwise, where the
fuses are not energized open; (2) breakers not marked "line" and "load" are suitable; (3)
breakers marked "line"/"load" may not be used for 705.30(A). **(E)** A backfed plug-in breaker may
omit the 408.36(D) hold-down fastener if the source is interactive-only, or a multimode inverter
with island-mode open-phase protection. (F) Transformers: the primary is the side toward the
largest fault source; the secondary is per 240.21(C).
- **Engine:** `code.NEC_705_30_source_ocpd` grades (B)'s floor and the ceiling (conductor at
