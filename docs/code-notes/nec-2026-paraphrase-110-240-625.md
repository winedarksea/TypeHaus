**110.26 Working space.**
- **Intro.** Working space and the way to it stay clear; open equipment doors must not impede
  access or egress. Access is impeded if all the doors open at once to 90 degrees leave less
  than 24 in. wide by 6 1/2 ft high.
- **(A) Equipment likely to be worked on live.** (1) Depth: 3 ft at 0-150 V to ground in
  every condition (151-600 V: 3, 3 1/2, 4 ft; 601-1000 V: 3, 4, 5 ft), measured from the
  enclosure front. Condition 1 live parts on one side only or guarded; 2 live parts one side,
  grounded (concrete, brick, tile walls included) the other; 3 live parts both sides.
  Dead-front assemblies need no rear or side space unless rear access is needed (30 in.).
  (2) Width: the equipment's, or 30 in., whichever is greater, always enough for a 90-degree
  door opening. (3) Height: floor to 6 1/2 ft or the equipment's height, whichever is
  greater; equipment above or below may stick out at most 6 in. Ex. 2: existing dwelling
  service equipment or panelboards of 200 A or less may have less than 6 1/2 ft. Ex. 3: meters
  in sockets may extend beyond. (4) Limited access: a 22 x 22 in. opening above a lay-in
  ceiling, 22 x 30 in. in a crawl space. (5) >1000 V equipment is separated. (6) The floor is
  level and clear.
- **(B)** No storage in the working space. **(C)** One entrance at least; two only for 1200 A
  and larger equipment over 6 ft wide; panic hardware only at 800 A or more (none for a dwelling).
- **(D) Illumination.** Working spaces about indoor service equipment, switchboards,
  switchgear, panelboards or MCCs are lit. All the luminaires may not be controlled by
  automatic means alone. No added outlet is needed if an adjacent source lights the space
  (or per 210.70(A)(1) Ex. 1).
- **(E) Dedicated space** for service equipment, switchboards, switchgear, panelboards and MCCs
  (Ex.: control equipment that must sit beside its machine). **(1) Indoors:** the equipment's
  width and depth, from the floor to 6 ft above it or the structural ceiling, whichever is
  lower, holds no piping, ducts, leak-protection apparatus or other foreign equipment
  (Ex.: a suspended ceiling with removable panels may sit in that 6 ft). Foreign systems above
  it are protected against condensation, leaks and breaks; sprinklers are allowed; a dropped
  ceiling is not a structural ceiling. **(2) Outdoors:** identified enclosures protected from
  unauthorized contact, vehicles and spillage; the (A) zone free of appurtenances and other
  equipment; and a dedicated space of the equipment's width and depth from grade to 6 ft above
  it, with no piping or foreign equipment (Ex.: structural overhangs and roof extensions).
- **(F)** A locked room still counts as accessible to qualified persons.
- **Engine:** `electrical.panel_working_space` grades (A)(1)-(3) for each panel: a 36 in. deep,
  30 in. wide (or the panel's), 78 in. high (or the panel's top, if higher) clear window
  that slides along the face. `electrical.panel_dedicated_space` grades (E)(1):
  a pipe or duct in the prism fails, conduit is allowed. Not graded: the door-swing sentence
  of the intro (an enclosure door opening to 90 degrees against the 24 x 6 1/2 ft clear
  path; the check's old TODO is answered by this text and stays a gap), (A)(4), (B), (C)
  (n/a to a dwelling) and (E)(1)(d). (D) graded by `electrical.working_space_lighting` (a
  luminaire in the panel's room not controlled only by occupancy or timer; none in the room is
  UNKNOWN for the adjacent-source allowance), Catlin four PASS. (E)(2) graded by
  `electrical.outdoor_working_space` (dedicated space from grade to 6 ft above the top free of
  pipe, duct and Equipment; (A) zone from grade, conduit excepted), `ED-M-METER` PASS. The
  door-swing sentence is still not graded (no door leaf or egress path).
- **Catlin.** All three panels (`ED-B-PANEL`, `-PANEL-2`, `ED-B-BACKUP-PANEL`) stand in
  `RM-B-FURNACE`, on the west wall. (D): that room has two cans
  (`ED-B-FURNACE-CAN1/2`, `CKT-LT-BACKUP`, switch `ED-B-FURNACE-SW`) and two strips
  (`-STRIP1/2`, `CKT-LT-BSMT`, switch `-SW2`), all on plain switches (`ED-T-LEV-SW1`), so the
  space is lit and manually controlled (`electrical.working_space_lighting`, four PASS). (E)(2): the meter-main is outdoors on the west
  wall. Nothing foreign (pipe, hydrant, gas, condenser, downspout) is authored within 6 ft of it;

  its axis), the service SPD (`ED-M-SPD`, 14 in.), the IBT and the data entry. Whether those

  passes it.

## Article 625: electric vehicle power transfer

Catlin has `CKT-EV-1450` (50 A, 2-pole) to a hardwired Grizzl-E (`ED-G-EV-1450`, 9,600 VA,
40 A), a 6-20R on `CKT-GAR-240` that `service_load` also files as EV, and a spare V2H raceway
(`CD-B-V2H-SPARE`/`CD-B-V2H-GAP`; `houses/catlin/notes/backup_power.md` section 10). This summarises the sections
the owner sent; their other detail is not restated.
- **625.40** Each outlet for EVSE above 16 A or above 120 V gets an individual branch circuit
  (Ex.: several EVSEs under 625.42(A)/(B)). **625.41** EVSE, bidirectional included, is a
  continuous load: a 40 A charger takes a 50 A circuit (125%). **625.42** The service, feeder and
  branch circuit are rated for the load unless limited by (A) a power control system per Article
  130 Part II (its maximum is the load; integral load management is marked; Ex.: fleet
  network software for three or more) or (B) an adjustable setting with restricted access per
  130.70(B), set at installation and field-labelled per 110.21.
- **625.43 Disconnect.** (C) Permanently connected EVSE has a readily accessible disconnect; if
  it is remote from the unit, a plaque on the unit says where. Cord-and-plug EVSE of 60 A or
  less, or 150 V or less to ground, may use its plug. The disconnect locks open (110.25).
  (D) Emergency shutoff applies only to other than one- and two-family dwellings (n/a). (B)
  covers multifamily.
- **625.44** A cord-and-plug attachment plug is rated at least 125% of the equipment (a 50 A
  receptacle may not go on a 40 A circuit); other EVSE is permanently wired and fixed.
  **625.46** No backfeed through the vehicle on loss of the primary source unless 625.48 applies.
  **625.47** More than one feeder or branch circuit is allowed where the equipment is identified for
  it. **625.48** Interactive or bidirectional EVSE is listed and marked for it; as optional
  standby, Article 702 applies, as a production source Article 705, and a receptacle export
  point 625.60. **625.49** Island mode is permitted. **625.50** The coupling means is stored at
  least 18 in. above an indoor floor or 24 in. above outdoor grade unless listed otherwise.
  **625.52** Ventilation: none if the equipment is listed for indoor charging without it,
  otherwise mechanical supply and exhaust, interlocked. **625.54** Every receptacle installed
  for EV charging is GFCI-protected. **625.60** An export receptacle on the vehicle is listed,
  250 V and 50 A at most, with OCPD and GFCI. Part IV, wireless charging (625.101-625.102): n/a.
- **Engine:** `electrical.evse_circuit` (advisory), for each circuit `service_load` files as EV
  (the description says EV charging; there is no EVSE device kind). It grades 625.40 (the
  circuit serves one device), 625.41 (`load_va` over 240 or 120 V, times 125%, against the
  breaker) and 625.54 (a receptacle on the circuit must be GFCI; none earns N/A), and reports
  625.43(C) for a hardwired unit; the plaque is derived when the EVSE's breaker panel is in
  another room, and `electrical.evse_circuit` still reports the advisory. 625.50 is graded from
  `ApplianceType.connector_holster_height`. 625.47 is open (no V2H charger chosen), not N/A.
  625.42, 625.44, 625.46, 625.48, 625.49, 625.52 and 625.60 are not graded.
- **Catlin.** `CKT-EV-1450`: serves only its box (625.40 passes); 9,600 VA / 240 V = 40 A x 125%
  = 50 A on a 50 A breaker (625.41 passes, no margin); hardwired with no receptacle, so
  625.54 is N/A. Its only disconnect is the breaker in `ED-B-PANEL-2`, in the basement and
  remote from the unit: **625.43(C) wants a plaque on the EVSE locating it, which is
  derived** (the check still reports the advisory, the same gap as the missing 225.31 garage disconnect). The unit's
  authored mount height is 42 in. above the garage floor, so a coupling means stored on it is
  above 625.50's 18 in. (graded, PASS). `CKT-GAR-240` (16 A x 125% = 20 A on 20 A; 6-20R, GFCI at the
  breaker) passes. **V2H:** the empty `CD-B-V2H-SPARE` raceway implies future bidirectional EVSE; 625.48
  needs it listed and marked, with 702 (standby) or 705 (production) and 625.47 if it takes its
  own feeder. Nothing grades that. Detail of 625.44-625.52 and the Catlin V2H reading are in
  `nec-2026-paraphrase-702-705-island.md`.
