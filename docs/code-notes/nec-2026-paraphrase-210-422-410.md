**210.18 Rating.** *(summary kept local)*
**Engine:** not graded (no circuit is above 50 A or 10 A).

**210.19 Conductors, minimum ampacity and size.** An informational note sizes for a 3% drop at the
farthest outlet, 5% with the feeder, and points at 215.4 for feeder drop.
- **(A)(1) Continuous loads.** The conductor ampacity is at least the noncontinuous load plus 125%
  of the continuous load (310.14), and also meets 110.14(C) at the terminations. Exception: where
  the assembly, OCPDs included, is listed for 100% operation, the conductor needs only the sum of
  the loads (110.14(C) still applies).
- **(A)(2)** The conductor is at least the maximum load served after adjustment and correction
  factors (310.15).
- **Exception to (1) and (2):** a branch circuit portion connected at both ends to separately
  installed pressure connections (110.14(C)(2)) may use the 310.15 allowable ampacity at the sum of the
  continuous and noncontinuous load, if it never enters an enclosure holding the circuit's supply
  or load terminations.
- **(B)** A circuit feeding more than one receptacle for cord-and-plug portable loads has
  conductors rated at least the circuit rating.
- **(C) Household ranges and cooking appliances.** The conductor is at least the circuit rating
  and the maximum load served; a range of 8¾ kW or more takes a 40 A circuit at the least. Ex. 1: taps
  not over 50 A to ranges, ovens and cooktops carry at least 20 A and the load, including leads
  smaller than the branch conductors, and are no longer than needed. Ex. 2: the neutral of a 3-wire range
  circuit may be smaller than the ungrounded conductors where the demand was computed by Table 120.55
  column C, but at least 70% of the circuit rating and not under 10 AWG.
- **(D) Other loads** use conductors sufficient for the load and no smaller than 14 AWG. Ex. 1: taps
  carry the load, are at least 15 A for circuits under 40 A and 20 A for 40 or 50 A circuits, and serve
  only: lampholders or luminaires with taps no longer than 18 in; a luminaire with tap conductors under
  410.117; outlets other than receptacles with taps up to 18 in; infrared heating appliances;
  nonheating deicing leads. Ex. 2: fixture wires and flexible cords may be under 14 AWG (240.5).
- **Engine:** (A)(1) graded in `electrical.continuous_load_circuit` on the authored
  `Circuit.continuous_va` split where present (breaker and the conductor's 110.14(C)(1) ampacity
  against noncontinuous + 125% continuous), else 125% of the whole connected VA; conductors are
  authored (`Circuit.conductor_awg`). (C): the same check's range branch.
  (A)(2), (B), (D) and the exceptions are not graded.

**210.20 Overcurrent protection.** Conductors and equipment take OCPDs rated per (A)-(D).
- **(A)** With continuous loads, the OCPD is at least the noncontinuous load plus 125% of the continuous.
  Exception: an assembly listed for 100% operation may use the plain sum.
- **(B)** Conductors are protected per 240.4; flexible cords and fixture wires per 240.5.
- **(C)** The OCPD rating or setting does not exceed what the equipment articles allow.
- **(D)** Nor what 210.21 allows for outlet devices.
- **Engine:** (A) graded in `electrical.continuous_load_circuit` on the authored
  `Circuit.continuous_va` split where present (breaker and the conductor's 110.14(C)(1) ampacity
  against noncontinuous + 125% continuous), else 125% of the whole connected VA; conductors are
  authored (`Circuit.conductor_awg`). (B)-(D) not graded here (see 440.22 and 424.4(B) for
  equipment limits).

**210.21 Outlet devices.** Each has an ampere rating at least the load served and meets (A) and (B).
- **(A) Lampholders** on a branch circuit over 20 A are heavy duty: at least 660 W (admedium type) or
  750 W (other types). **Engine:** not graded (no such circuit).
- **(B)(1) A single receptacle on an individual circuit** is rated at least the circuit. Ex. 1:
  motor receptacles under 430.81(B). Ex. 2: a receptacle only for a cord-and-plug arc welder may
  match the welder's minimum conductor ampacity (630.11(A)).
- **(B)(2) Total cord-and-plug load.** On a circuit with two or more receptacles, a receptacle does
  not carry more than Table 210.21(B)(2): circuit 15 or 20 A with a 15 A receptacle, 12 A; 20 A
  circuit with a 20 A receptacle, 16 A; 30 A with a 30 A receptacle, 24 A.
- **(B)(3) Receptacle ratings** on a multiple-receptacle circuit follow Table 210.21(B)(3), or the
  circuit rating above 50 A. The table (read 2026-11-14): 15 A circuit takes 15 A receptacles; 20 A
  takes 15 or 20; 30 takes 30; 40 takes 40 or 50; 50 takes 50. Ex. 1: arc welder receptacles. Ex. 2: a receptacle
  for electric-discharge lighting may be rated under 410.62(C).
- **(B)(4)** A range receptacle's rating may follow one range's demand load (120.55).
- **Engine:** (B)(1) in `electrical.single_receptacle_rating`, off the NEMA configuration on the
  device type (a combination device is skipped). (B)(3) in `electrical.multi_receptacle_rating`
  (ADVISORY): all 12 circuits PASS; a deadfront GFCI is not a receptacle. (B)(2) is not
  graded: the model has no cord-and-plug load per receptacle.

**210.22-210.25 (read 2026-11-14).** **210.22** An individual circuit may serve any load it is rated
for; a 10 A individual circuit may not serve receptacles (checked in `multi_receptacle_rating`).
**210.23** Permissible loads: (A) 10 A circuits, lighting outlets or a dwelling's bath/laundry exhaust
fans on a lighting circuit; (B) 15 and 20 A: (1) one cord-and-plug item not fastened in place at

8/12, 50 A 6/12 AWG (receptacle ratings as Table 210.21(B)(3)). **210.25** multi-occupancy only:
N/A for a dwelling.
- **Engine:** (B)(1)/(B)(2) graded by `electrical.permissible_loads` off
  `EquipmentType.utilization_connection`; `CKT-WH` 80.0% PASS, fireplace/garage/plant heaters
  62.5% PASS, ERV and HP3 fastened alone. Table 210.24(1): sizes now authored and match.

## 210.70 Lighting outlets (dwellings and guest rooms)

**(A)(1)** Every habitable room, kitchen, laundry area and bathroom has at least one lighting outlet
controlled by a wall switch or listed wall-mounted control, near an entrance on a wall. Ex. 1: outside
kitchens, laundry areas and bathrooms, one or more switched receptacles may stand in for lighting
outlets. Ex. 2: occupancy sensors may control them if in addition to wall switches, or at a usual
switch location with a manual override. Ex. 3: a laundry's lighting outlet and switch may be outside
the laundry where a closet houses the equipment.
**Engine:** `electrical.room_lighting` and
`electrical.lighting_controls` (a light and a switch per room, and every named switch resolves).

**(A)(2) Additional locations.**
- **(1)** At least one switched lighting outlet in hallways, stairways, attached garages, detached
  garages and accessory buildings with power.
- **(2)** Dwelling units, attached garages and powered detached garages: at least one exterior lighting
  outlet on a wall switch or listed control at outdoor entrances or exits with grade-level access
  (a garage vehicle door does not count). Exception: for an outdoor grade-level bulkhead door to a
  sub-grade basement stair, the light may be inside the basement within 1.5 m (5 ft) horizontally
  of the bottom riser, controlled by a switch, listed control or the luminaire's unit switch.
- **(3)** An interior stair with six or more risers between floor levels has a wall switch or listed
  control at each floor level and each landing level that has a stair entry.
- **(4)** *(New in 2026.)* A dimmer on the (3) lighting is permitted only if the listed control can reach
  full brightness at every control location of the stair.
- **Exception to (1), (2), (3):** remote, central or automatic control may be used in hallways,
  stairways and at outdoor entrances.
- **Engine:** (1) and (2) are not graded as such (`electrical.room_lighting` takes habitable rooms,
  equipment spaces and storage with equipment, not hallways, stairs or exterior entrances).
  (3) `electrical.stair_lighting_control`, off `serves_stair`: one luminaire switched within
  10 ft of each end's nosing on each level; landings, the automatic-control exception and (4)
  not graded.

**(B) Guest rooms and suites** (hotels, motels and similar). At least one switched lighting outlet in
each habitable room and bathroom, with the same two exceptions about switched receptacles and occupancy
sensors.
**Engine:** not applicable.

**410.10 Luminaires in specific locations.**
- **(A) Wet and damp.** Installed so water cannot enter or collect in wiring compartments,
  lampholders or other electrical parts. A luminaire in a wet location is marked for wet locations; one
  in a damp location is marked for wet or damp. **Engine:** `electrical.wet_location` (bathrooms and
  the exterior: damp or wet rated).
- **(B) Corrosive locations** take luminaires suitable for them. **(C) Ducts or hoods:** allowed in a
  commercial cooking hood if identified for it, sealed against vapors, corrosion-resistant with a smooth
  face and wired outside the hood. Not applicable here.
- **(D) Bathtub and shower areas.**
  - **(1)** No part of a cord-connected luminaire, chain-, cable- or cord-suspended luminaire, lighting
    track, pendant, or ceiling-suspended (paddle) fan with a light kit is within 3 ft horizontally and
    8 ft (2.5 m) vertically of the tub rim or shower threshold. The zone is all-encompassing and
    includes the space directly over the tub or shower.
  - **(2)** A luminaire within the tub or shower's outside dimension, up to 8 ft above the rim or
    threshold, is marked suitable for damp (or wet) locations; one subject to shower spray is wet.
  - **Engine:** `electrical.bath_luminaire_zone`. (1): forms track, pendant, chandelier or
    `ceiling_fan_light` within the zone and in the tub's own room (`room_owning`): FAIL at 120 V,
    UNKNOWN at 30 V or under (a violation; Article 411 exempts nothing; held for the gate). One in
    another room PASSes (a wall separates it); an unresolved room is UNKNOWN.
    Cord-connected luminaires are not modelled. (2): wet rated passes; damp only or unrated is UNKNOWN
    (whether spray reaches it is not modelled). The rim is the tub body top for a `-TUB`, and the pan
    floor for a shower (conservative). The 3 ft is measured from the footprint; a luminaire on another
    storey is outside the zone.
- **(E) Indoor sports, mixed-use and all-purpose facilities:** luminaires using mercury-vapour or
  metal-halide lamps where subject to damage have a glass or plastic lens guard. Not applicable.
- **(F) Under roof decking.** A luminaire where subject to physical damage is installed and supported
  at least 38 mm (1½ in) from the lowest surface of the roof decking to the top of the luminaire.
  Exception: metal-corrugated roof decking covered with at least 50 mm (2 in) of concrete. **Engine:**
  not graded; Catlin's attic cans sit under a roof deck, so the 1½ in is a rough-in item.
